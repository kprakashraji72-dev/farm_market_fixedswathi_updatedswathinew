{# FAQ Item 1 #}

Once your order is dispatched, our GPS-enabled driver broadcasts live road coordinates. You can open your Product Delivery tracking page anytime to watch the rider route in real time.
{# FAQ Item 2 #}

To ensure secure physical handover, an OTP code appears on your live tracking page the moment your driver arrives. Share this code with the rider only after you inspect and receive your produce.
{# FAQ Item 3 #}

All our fresh vegetables and fruits are sourced directly from certified organic partner farms with ISO harvest verification and delivered within hours of harvest to minimize shelf storage.
{# FAQ Item 4 #}

If any product fails to meet your quality expectations, select Product Quality in the support form on the right and select your order number. Our team will issue a refund or free replacement within 24 hours.
{# FAQ Item 5 #}

Our fleet operates daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Same-day delivery is available for all orders placed before 5:00 PM.
{# FAQ Item 6 #}

You can update your contact phone, preferred delivery address, and account details anytime under your Account Settings.
